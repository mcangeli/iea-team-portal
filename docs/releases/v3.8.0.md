# ArenaLine v3.8.0 — Barn, Facility & Resource Operations

Released September 2026.

## Summary

v3.8.0 establishes ArenaLine's canonical physical-operations layer. It introduces organization-scoped facilities and nested spaces, independent horse stall/turnout assignments, reservable physical resources for lesson scheduling, and inventory stock operations tied to storage-capable spaces.

The release deliberately preserves existing domain ownership. Facilities identify physical places; Lessons own lesson facts; Horses own horse records; Inventory owns stock facts; Calendar remains a projection; Finance/AP remains authoritative for vendors, obligations, payments, and financial transactions.

## Facility & Resource Foundation

- Adds `Facility` as the organization-scoped site/property identity.
- Adds nestable `FacilitySpace` records for barns/buildings, arenas/rings, stalls, pastures/paddocks, feed rooms, tack rooms, storage, wash/grooming areas, and other durable places.
- Uses capabilities rather than separate resource models for reservable, stall/housing, pasture/turnout, and inventory-storage behavior.
- Enforces organization/facility parent boundaries and rejects cyclic hierarchies.
- Provides hierarchical Facilities UI and deterministic parent/child navigation.

## Stall Housing & Pasture Turnout

- Adds effective-dated stall assignments with preserved housing history.
- Adds effective-dated pasture/turnout assignments with preserved turnout history.
- Stall housing and pasture turnout are independent relationships.
- A horse may simultaneously have a stall and pasture assignment, have pasture boarding without a stall, or temporarily have neither.
- Assignment validation keeps horses and spaces inside the same organization and requires the appropriate space capability.

## Lesson Resource Scheduling

- Adds reservations for reservable Facility Spaces.
- Lesson occurrences may reserve canonical resources without moving lesson ownership into Facilities.
- Reservation services enforce time/resource compatibility and preserve source provenance.
- Existing free-text lesson/calendar/show locations remain valid compatibility and historical snapshots.
- External show venues continue to work without requiring local Facility records.

## Inventory Operations

- Adds organization-scoped inventory categories and items.
- Tracks stock quantities by storage-capable Facility Space.
- Supports receive, use, count adjustment, and transfer operations.
- Preserves durable transaction history and adjustment direction.
- Surfaces total on hand, location counts, low-stock status, stock by location, and recent history.
- Keeps Inventory stock facts separate from Facility identity and Finance/AP accounting ownership.
- Adds polished Inventory workspace, item detail, movement forms, and direct navigation to storage-location management.

## Compatibility

This release is additive. It does not remove or reinterpret established free-text location/venue fields, legacy lesson structures, external show venues, or existing finance ownership. Historical location text remains usable when no canonical Facility Space is linked.

The v3.4 rider self-rescheduling test fixture was also made wall-calendar independent after release validation exposed a hard-coded September 2026 date boundary. No production behavior was loosened to satisfy the test.
