# ArenaLine v3.5.0 — Barn Finance & Business Operations

Released September 2026.

ArenaLine v3.5.0 establishes a generic operational-finance architecture for barn and equestrian organizations while preserving the existing IEA finance workflows and keeping IEA-specific behavior outside the generic finance core.

## Finance domains and authorization

General Barn finance and IEA finance are explicit, separate domains. Access is resolved through finance capabilities and organization scope.

Administrators and users with Manage All Finance may operate both domains. Manage IEA Finance and compatible legacy IEA Treasurer authority remain IEA-only. Finance services, reconciliation, reports, exports, and navigation all enforce these boundaries server-side.

## Receivables

The generic receivables model is:

```text
Receivable Account
  → Charge / Credit
  → Payment
  → Allocation
  → Balance / aging / reporting
```

Accounts support billing relationships and statement-recipient designation. Charges, credits, and payments preserve posted/void lifecycle behavior. Payments and credits can be allocated to charges, explicitly unallocated for corrections, and reallocated without deleting financial history. Removing an account-person relationship is a soft deactivation so historical billing relationships remain durable.

Cross-account and cross-domain allocation boundaries are validated rather than relying on presentation controls.

## Bank imports and reconciliation

Bank reconciliation supports configurable CSV/XLSX import profiles tied to an authorized financial account. Imported rows are staged records and do not create or alter ledger transactions.

Candidate generation looks for compatible posted ledger transactions by account, direction, amount, date proximity, reference, and descriptive similarity. A candidate remains only a suggestion until explicitly confirmed. One bank row and one ledger transaction cannot be multiply confirmed.

Rows may be explicitly ignored when no ledger reconciliation is appropriate. Resolving the final row moves a batch to Reviewed; the batch reaches Completed only through the explicit Complete Review action.

## Accounting exports

Accounting Export Profiles provide configurable CSV/XLSX projections of authorized posted ArenaLine ledger activity. QuickBooks-friendly column mappings are supported without making an external accounting package authoritative inside ArenaLine.

Exports are read-only and do not mark transactions synchronized, exported, reconciled, or otherwise mutate ledger state.

## Business reporting

The Finance Reporting workspace provides:

- posted income, expenses, and net movement;
- monthly cash-flow trends;
- activity by category and financial account;
- open receivable totals and overdue totals;
- receivable aging buckets and account-level drill-down;
- General/IEA domain selection subject to authorization;
- season and transaction-date filters;
- receivable as-of reporting;
- CSV report export.

Point-in-time receivable reports exclude charges whose charge date is later than the selected as-of date. Aging labels use Current, 1–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, and 90+ days.

Historical as-of reporting uses the persisted current allocation state; v3.5 does not claim to reconstruct a separate historical general-ledger balance at every past instant.

## Navigation and compatibility

The primary Finance navigation now exposes the generic Finance workspace, Business Reporting, Bank Reconciliation, and Accounting Exports to authorized finance users. IEA-authorized users retain the established IEA finance workflows where applicable.

Existing IEA dues, family receivables, budgets, fundraising, reimbursements, and related compatibility workflows remain available rather than being destructively migrated into the new generic models.

## Presentation

New v3.5 Finance surfaces use the ArenaLine premium equestrian presentation system, shared typography/components, responsive layouts, and theme-aware controls. Finance forms and report/export workspaces include explicit dark-mode treatment and mobile layouts.

## Migrations and compatibility

v3.5 finance migrations begin at `0090` and extend through `0099_v350_accounting_export_profiles.py`, covering the finance foundation, receivables/business operations, bank-import/reconciliation persistence, and accounting export profiles.

The release is compatibility-first: existing financial history and legacy IEA workflows are preserved. Generic finance services are the preferred boundary for new Barn Finance work.
