# ArenaLine v3.9.0 — People & Rider Architecture

**Status:** Release candidate. Production promotion pending.

## Summary

v3.9.0 completes the transition to Person as ArenaLine's canonical human identity. A login is optional; roles, family relationships, program participation, IEA membership, horse relationships, and financial account relationships attach to Person without creating duplicate human records.

## Delivered

- Person-native organization/group participation and IEA participant identity.
- Person-native IEA season membership, competition participation, Rider Card projections, and normal roster workflows.
- Compatibility adapters for historical Rider and Guardian records, bookmarks, and related forms.
- Person-native family relationships and account management; no inference of financial responsibility from family or riding relationships.
- Person-native generic operational participation, with historical Rider references retained where necessary.
- Explicit isolation of the legacy lesson stack from the canonical Lesson Program architecture.
- Historical ledger Rider attribution retained as deprecated compatibility metadata; receivable account relationships remain financially authoritative.

## Compatibility and migration

This is an additive migration. Legacy Rider, Guardian, lesson, and ledger records are **not** destructively removed. Existing data is bridged deterministically where supported; historical facts remain historical. See `docs/V3_9_0_PEOPLE_RIDER_ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/V3_9_0_COMPATIBILITY_CLOSEOUT.md`.

Migrations 0115–0126 introduce Person-native participation links and compatibility backfills. Deploy through the normal `portalctl update` stable release path after promotion, which performs backup and preflight checks.
