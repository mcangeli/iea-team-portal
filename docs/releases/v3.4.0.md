# ArenaLine v3.4.0 — Lesson Program

Released September 2026.

ArenaLine v3.4.0 replaces the older lesson-only assumptions with a generic lesson-program architecture that supports both everyday barn instruction and IEA team lessons while preserving historical/legacy lesson records for compatibility.

## Core lesson architecture

The generic hierarchy is:

```text
Lesson Program
  └── Lesson Series
       ├── Lesson Enrollment → Person
       └── Lesson Occurrence
            ├── Attendance → Person
            └── Assignment → Person / Horse
```

Programs describe the offering, series describe recurring instructional groups, occurrences are durable scheduled instances, enrollment describes normal membership, attendance records what happened, and assignments capture operational Person/Horse participation.

Horse and Person remain canonical ArenaLine entities. Lesson history snapshots instructor, location, capacity, title, and schedule identity so later edits to a recurring series do not rewrite past occurrences.

## Barn and IEA lesson boundaries

Barn lesson programs use `LessonEnrollment` for roster membership and restrict instructional assignment to active canonical Trainer / Assistant Trainer roles.

IEA Team Lessons use season/team context rather than a generic lesson type. Their roster comes from existing season membership, bridged to canonical Person identity, and instructional assignment is restricted to Coaches. Futures and Upper School team context remains explicit.

Legacy IEA lessons can be converted into the new occurrence model with durable provenance while the original records remain unchanged for compatibility. Conversion is deterministic and idempotent.

## Scheduling and preparation

v3.4.0 adds recurring occurrence generation, one-off occurrences, safe future schedule refresh, cancellation/rescheduling behavior, immutable recurrence identity, occurrence snapshots, and idempotent roster preparation.

Generated occurrences preserve their original scheduled slot even after rescheduling. Manual and converted legacy occurrences are explicitly distinguished from recurrence-generated occurrences.

Preparation materializes expected participants and instructor assignments without confusing enrollment with attendance or creating instructor attendance rows.

## Lesson-day operations

Authorized staff can operate an occurrence from a lesson-day workspace, including participant attendance, horse assignment, bulk updates, rescheduling, and completion.

Completion is blocked while expected attendance remains unresolved and cannot be applied to cancelled lessons. Completion is idempotent and does not invent a horse-assignment requirement.

Barn lesson management is available to Administrators and active Trainers / Assistant Trainers. IEA Team Lesson management is available to Administrators and Coaches. Domain-specific instructor eligibility is enforced in forms and model validation.

## Rider moves and make-ups

A single rider can be moved to a compatible upcoming lesson without rescheduling the entire occurrence. The original lesson retains historical provenance and the destination records make-up participation.

Compatibility rules keep Barn moves inside the same lesson program and IEA moves inside the same season/team level. Capacity, destination history, occurrence status, duplicate moves, and attendance conflicts are validated. A database constraint prevents more than one active move record from the same source occurrence for the same Person.

Riders with appropriate canonical or legacy rider identity may reschedule only themselves. Staff-initiated and rider-initiated changes retain audit provenance.

## Unified calendar

The operational calendar projects domain-owned records rather than becoming a second source of truth. v3.4 includes Barn lessons, IEA lessons, legacy IEA compatibility, shows, horse-care activity, organization events, volunteer/committee activity, and other/manual events where supported. Calendar links route back to the owning domain.

Legacy IEA provenance prevents converted lessons from appearing twice in the unified calendar.

## Navigation and presentation

Navigation now separates Barn Lesson Programs from IEA Team Lessons while preserving role-aware access. Riders receive My Lessons when eligible. Existing Finance navigation remains available and unchanged in scope pending the dedicated v3.5 finance release.

Lesson Program, series, occurrence, lesson-day, move/make-up, My Lessons, and calendar surfaces use the established ArenaLine responsive light/dark presentation system.

## Compatibility and migrations

v3.4 remains compatibility-first. Existing legacy lesson structures remain in place while new generic lesson records operate alongside them. The release includes migrations `0079_v340_lesson_program_foundation.py` through `0089_v340_lesson_model_state_closeout.py`.

The final migrations include explicit recurrence identity, IEA context/provenance, participant-move audit and uniqueness, and migration-state alignment. The final release gate reports no model/migration drift.
