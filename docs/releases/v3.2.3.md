# ArenaLine v3.2.3 — People & Operations Polish

Released September 2026.

v3.2.3 closes the ArenaLine 3.2.x People, relationships, barn participation, and Station release family. It consolidates the compatibility-first People architecture introduced across v3.2.0–v3.2.2 and hardens the operational experience before v3.3 Equine Care & Horse Management.

## Highlights

### Unified People and access

- Canonical organization-scoped `Person` identity remains distinct from login access.
- Person-first login creation and management keeps credentials separate from human identity and participation.
- Existing Rider, GuardianContact, UserProfile, SeasonMembership, finance, competition, and historical references remain compatibility structures rather than being destructively rewritten.
- Family linking can reuse existing People or existing organization login accounts without creating duplicate identities.
- Parent/guardian access follows effective-dated canonical People relationships, with compatibility fallbacks retained where required.

### Roles, groups, committees, and Barn Operations

- Multiple concurrent organization roles remain supported without turning role labels into permissions.
- Current-role selection honors effective dates using `start_date <= today < end_date`.
- Barn Operations applies the same effective-date boundary to organization roles and horse-person relationships.
- Committee dashboards and membership presentation honor current effective dates.
- Person-scoped committee membership forms preserve the Person selected by the route rather than requiring a second identity selection.

### ArenaLine Station and work history

- Shared-device Station workflows retain separate Station credentials/PINs and tenant-safe work-shift records.
- Managers can review, correct, approve, and export work history.
- Station review queues are built server-side as explicit attention and approved queues.
- Person profiles expose privacy-aware work history and Working Student time to managers and the Person themself.

### Public rider profiles

- Public rider cards and profile presentation were refined for a stronger ArenaLine public experience.
- Anonymous rider publication remains explicit opt-in and allow-listed.
- Public fields are limited to approved profile data such as display identity, photo, bio, website, Instagram, and YouTube.
- Private contact, birth-date, family, role, and other internal data remain outside the public payload.

### Presentation and accessibility

- Shared navigation and page-shell keyboard behavior were hardened.
- Mobile/tablet behavior, focus treatment, touch targets, table overflow, reduced-motion handling, and responsive rider-card presentation were refined.
- Existing ArenaLine light/dark presentation conventions remain intact.

## Compatibility and privacy

v3.2.3 remains compatibility-first. Existing IEA-specific terminology and behavior—including Futures/Upper, IEA classes, points riders, Hoofprint, qualification, and postseason workflows—remain domain-specific and are not genericized by the People architecture.

Authorization remains server-side. Organizational roles, relationships, permissions, and public identity are separate concepts.
