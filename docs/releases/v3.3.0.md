# ArenaLine v3.3.0 — Equine Care & Horse Management

Released September 2026.

v3.3.0 expands ArenaLine's existing competition-oriented Horse Registry into an operational equine-care, compliance, and delegated horse-management system while preserving the established IEA Horse, Show, Hoofprint, Horse of the Day, and historical workflows.

## Highlights

### Horse registry and identity

- Horses remain first-class organization-scoped ArenaLine records rather than show-only resources.
- Added durable horse identifiers for organization, registry, and external-authority identifiers without overloading the core Horse record.
- Expanded People↔Horse care relationships to support veterinarian, farrier, dentist, and emergency-contact roles alongside ownership, boarding, lease, trainer, responsible-party, and caretaker relationships.
- Existing season eligibility, show assignments, awards, and historical records remain compatibility-safe.

### Equine care records and scheduling

- Added organization-scoped horse care records for vaccination, farrier, dental, veterinary, medication, wellness, and other care activity.
- Care records preserve performed date, provider, notes, and optional next-due date so completed history is not overwritten by current state.
- Added manager-only care entry/editing and a full completed-care history organized by year.
- Added care scheduling intelligence using the latest record in each care category, with overdue, due-soon, and current status.
- Coggins remains a specialized record rather than being duplicated as generic care data.

### Documents and compliance

- Added generic horse documents for registration, vaccination, lease, ownership, veterinary, insurance, care, and other supporting records.
- Documents support effective/expiration dates, optional care-record linkage, notes, and expiration status.
- Added configurable organization horse-compliance requirements for Coggins and selected document types.
- Compliance identifies missing, expired, expiring-soon, and current requirements without assuming every stored document type is mandatory.
- Generic horse documents and Coggins attachments are served through authenticated, organization-scoped horse-management download views with private/no-store response headers.

### Delegated horse management

- Added an explicit organization capability model, beginning with **Manage Horses**, so horse-management authority does not have to be represented as a Coach/Admin job title.
- Admins and Coaches retain their established horse-management authority for backward compatibility.
- A Person/account granted **Manage Horses** receives organization-wide Horse Registry management without receiving People, Finance, competition, account, or organization administration authority.
- A Person with a current Boarder / Responsible Party relationship receives horse-management access only to the related horse.
- Relationship-derived access honors active/effective dates, so ending the relationship removes the elevated horse access.
- Scoped horse managers can maintain horse profile information, identifiers, care records/history, Coggins, documents, protected downloads, and horse compliance visibility.
- Relationship administration, season eligibility, show assignments, Horse of the Day, and organization-wide compliance-requirement configuration remain separately restricted. A relationship-derived manager cannot modify the relationship that grants the access.

### Show readiness and Hoofprint

- Show compliance is evaluated through the actual show date rather than only against today's date.
- A record that is valid today but expires before a show is treated as not valid through the show date.
- Expiration on the show date itself remains valid.
- Missing, expired, or not-valid-through-show required records block Show Readiness; expiring-soon records warn without blocking when they remain valid through the show.
- Show Horses surfaces show-date compliance details to authorized horse managers while preserving Coggins-only visibility for non-managers.
- Hoofprint Builder surfaces exact compliance blockers and prevents finalization while required horse compliance is unresolved.
- Hoofprint preview remains available and ordinary Hoofprint completeness warnings remain advisory.

### Privacy and compatibility

- Care, document, and configurable compliance details remain restricted to authorized horse managers unless a workflow deliberately exposes a narrower compatibility-safe field.
- Non-manager show-horse presentation retains established Coggins behavior without exposing private document requirement names or care information.
- Existing IEA-specific Horse/Hoofprint terminology remains on IEA-specific surfaces.
- Historical Horse of the Day removal remains POST-only and the established `show_horse_award_remove` URL/handler contract is preserved.
