# ArenaLine v3.7.0 — Barn Finance Completion

ArenaLine v3.7.0 completes the operational Barn Finance program introduced in v3.5 while preserving established IEA finance workflows and the existing FinancialTransaction ledger.

## Release scope

### Accounts payable

- Domain-separated General Barn and IEA payees/vendors.
- Payable obligations with due dates, references, expense categories, seasons, lifecycle status, and balances.
- Posted and voided payments integrated with FinancialTransaction.
- Payable aging, workspace summaries, reporting, and CSV export.

### Receivables completion

- Receivables workspace and account lifecycle presentation.
- Recurring/service billing rules and idempotent generation.
- Lesson billing adapters.
- Earned-credit rules for supported Barn work and lesson horse-use activity.
- Oldest-first payment and credit allocation.
- Statement and correction workflows.
- Closed receivable accounts are read-only for normal new activity while historical correction/void workflows remain available.

### Barn budgeting

- Generic Budget and BudgetLine architecture for both General Barn and IEA finance domains.
- Planned income and expenses mapped to existing FinancialCategory records.
- Actuals calculated from FinancialTransaction; no second budgeting ledger.
- Planned-versus-actual and variance reporting in budget and unified finance surfaces.
- Closed-budget line mutation protection.

### Operations and reporting

- Unified Finance Workspace with receivables, payables, active budgets, exceptions, and recent ledger activity.
- Unified finance reporting with AR/AP balances, aging, budget performance, category/account summaries, and CSV export.
- Transaction drill-down and source traceability.
- Historical effective-date AR/AP balances for report as-of dates.
- Bank reconciliation and accounting-export workflows remain integrated with the same operational ledger.

## Compatibility

General Barn and IEA finance remain separate authorization domains.

Legacy IEA finance remains available. SeasonBudget and ShowBudgetLine records are mirrored non-destructively into the generic budget architecture. Generic-only planning lines are preserved. When multiple legacy show budget rows share the same category and transaction kind, the compatibility bridge aggregates their amounts into the single generic BudgetLine allowed by the v3.7 model.

Historical as-of reporting is an effective-date position using records that are currently valid. ArenaLine does not currently maintain separate effective-date history for later void/status changes, so v3.7.0 should not be treated as an immutable audit-time accounting snapshot.

## Migrations

v3.7 begins with `0102_v370_accounts_payable_foundation.py` and includes receivables and budgeting migrations through `0109`.

Existing legacy finance data is preserved; v3.7 does not replace or destructively migrate the legacy IEA finance subsystem.
