# ArenaLine v3.8.0 Preview 1 — Facility & Resource Foundation

Status: architecture locked for Preview 1; regression boundary before migration.

## Purpose

Introduce the canonical physical-operations foundation for ArenaLine without moving ownership of Lessons, Shows, Horses, Calendar, Station, or Finance into a new scheduling silo.

## Inventory findings

The v3.7 baseline stores several physical-place references as snapshots/free text:
- CalendarEvent.location
- LessonSeries.default_location
- LessonOccurrence.location
- Show.venue / Show.address
- ShowClass.schedule_note
- Horse.home_barn

These fields remain valid compatibility/history surfaces. External show venues must continue to work without a local Facility record.

ArenaLine Station owns shared-device identity and WorkShiftEntry, not physical facilities. Finance already owns vendors/payables through PayableParty -> PayableObligation -> PayablePayment -> FinancialTransaction.

## Canonical physical model

### Facility
An organization-scoped physical site/property/campus. Examples: Main Farm, Show Grounds, Satellite Barn.

### FacilitySpace
A durable named place within a Facility. Spaces may nest.

Initial space types:
- barn/building
- arena/ring
- stall
- pasture/paddock
- feed room
- tack room
- storage
- wash/grooming area
- other

A FacilitySpace is a physical identity first. Capabilities determine which operational workflows may use it; a place does not become a separate model merely because it can be reserved, house a horse, support turnout, or store inventory.

Initial capabilities:
- reservable
- stall/housing
- pasture/turnout
- inventory storage

Parent/child spaces must belong to the same Facility and organization. Cycles are forbidden.

## Locked horse-assignment rule

Stall housing and pasture turnout are independent relationships. A stall is never tied to a pasture.

A normally stalled horse may simultaneously have:
- HorseStallAssignment -> Stall
- HorsePastureAssignment -> Pasture/Paddock

A pasture-board horse may have a pasture assignment and no stall assignment. A horse may temporarily have neither.

### HorseStallAssignment
Effective-dated assignment of Horse to a stall-capable FacilitySpace. Preserves housing history.

### HorsePastureAssignment
Effective-dated assignment of Horse to a pasture/turnout-capable FacilitySpace. Preserves turnout history.

Pasture-specific operations such as rotation/rest periods, turnout groups, inspections, fencing/water status, and maintenance are later 3.8.2 records and must not mutate stall housing.

## Resource reservation boundary

ResourceReservation will reserve a reservable FacilitySpace for a time interval and retain source/provenance sufficient to associate the reservation with the owning operational activity.

The reservation layer does not become authoritative for Lesson, Show, Calendar, or other activity facts. Those domains own their records; scheduling consumes/projects them.

Preview 1 establishes the resource identity/reservation boundary. Cross-domain collision policy is completed in 3.8.3.

## Compatibility rules

1. Do not remove or reinterpret existing free-text location/venue fields in Preview 1.
2. Canonical FacilitySpace links are additive.
3. Historical text remains a display/snapshot fallback when no canonical space is linked.
4. External venues do not require Facility records.
5. No automatic conversion of ambiguous location strings into FacilitySpace records.
6. Existing Calendar projection remains an aggregation/presentation surface.
7. Station may later execute facility operations but does not own Facility data.
8. Inventory may use storage-capable FacilitySpaces, but Inventory owns stock facts.
9. Purchasing may link to PayableParty/PayableObligation; Finance remains authoritative for AP and financial transactions.

## Preview 1 regression boundary

Tests must establish before UI work:
- Facility is organization scoped.
- FacilitySpace cannot cross organizations/facilities through parent relationships.
- FacilitySpace parent hierarchy rejects self-parent/cycles.
- stall assignments accept only stall/housing-capable spaces.
- pasture assignments accept only pasture/turnout-capable spaces.
- horse and assigned space must belong to the same organization.
- stall and pasture assignments are independent.
- pasture-board horse is valid with no stall.
- effective-date ranges reject end-before-start.
- overlapping active stall assignments for one horse are rejected.
- overlapping active primary pasture assignments for one horse are rejected while future turnout-group design remains possible.
- ResourceReservation accepts only reservable spaces.
- reservation end must be after start.
- existing lesson/calendar/show free-text location behavior remains valid.
- external show venue behavior remains valid.
- existing Station work-shift behavior remains unchanged.
- no Finance/AP model duplication is introduced.

## Preview sequence

3. Migration 0110 and model implementation.
4. Admin/basic registry surfaces.
5. Compatibility links for location-aware domains only after the foundation is green.
6. Full regression suite before advancing to 3.8.1 Stall & Horse Housing.
